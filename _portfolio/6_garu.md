NASA's Curiosity rover has found evidence for ancient long-lived lakes in Gale crater, Mars. It is important to contextualize these observations by investigating whether river and lake systems, and the hydrologic conditions that form them, were common in the region or whether Gale is excpetional. We investigated canyons and sedimentary fans in Garu crater, 150 km East of Gale to constrain how much water was needed and when they formed. Our results indicate that semi-arid conditions could have been widespread in the Gale region during the later phases of aqueous activity at Gale.
