As a Sophomore and Junior Science Scholar at Dartmouth working with Dr. Meredith Kelly, I led the investigation of sediment layer in local lake cores. This layer sat immediately above varves from the glacial lake Hitchcock that had filled the Connecticut river valley and had been thought to be remobilized varve sediment, but showed different magnetic susceptibility. Through looking at sediment under the SEM, I observed that variations in sediment magnetic susceptibiliity were correlated with the presence or absence of frambiodal pyrite that indicated reducing conditions. I hypothesized that magnetic susceptibility reflected local lake redox variations in addition to provenence, which it had been previously assumed to record in work on these cores.
