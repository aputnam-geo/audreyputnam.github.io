As part of the DIGMARS team, I flew a drone to produce orthomosaics and digital elevation models of delta and shoreline locations that were cored by the DIGMARS team. This allowed my collaborator Joe Tamborski to do detailed modelling of groundwater moving and mixing with groundwater. In addition, I have taught a lecture and demonstration on drones and photogrammetry for a remote sensing course at Rice and gave demonstrations of drone-based mapping and science for Rice University outreach events.
