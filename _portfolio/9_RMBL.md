As a Summer undergraduate research fellow at the Rocky Mountain Biological Laboratory in Gothic, Colorado, I  collected data on mountain white crowned sparrows. I compared several years of nesting success data to local records of temperature and precipitation to quantify the effects that chaning climate may have on these birds. Additionally, I trapped, banded, and collected data from many adult birds for future studies.
